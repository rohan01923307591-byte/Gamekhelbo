GTA 6 Release Date: Everything We Know So Far

Grand Theft Auto VI is one of the most anticipated games in the world. Here is a clear summary of the latest information officially confirmed by Rockstar Games.

GTA 6 Release Date

Rockstar Games currently lists November 19, 2026 as the release date for Grand Theft Auto VI. The game is officially announced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Which Platforms Will Get GTA 6?

The currently announced platforms are PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC release date, so unofficial dates and rumors should be treated as unconfirmed.

Where Is GTA 6 Set?

The game returns to the fictional state of Leonida, including a modern version of Vice City. Rockstar describes the setting as a major evolution of the series' open-world experience.

Who Are Jason and Lucia?

Jason and Lucia are the two central characters featured in Rockstar's official GTA VI material. Their story follows a dangerous situation that develops across Leonida, making their partnership an important part of the game's narrative.

What About Pre-Orders and Pricing?

Rockstar announced pre-orders in June 2026. The Standard Edition was announced at $79.99, while the Ultimate Edition was announced at $99.99. Regional pricing can differ.

What About GTA 6 on PC?

As of this update, Rockstar has not officially announced a PC release date. If Rockstar announces one, GameKhelbo can update this article with the confirmed information.

Final Thoughts

GTA VI is currently scheduled for November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. With Vice City, Leonida, Jason and Lucia, Rockstar is preparing a major new chapter in the Grand Theft Auto series.

Source note: This article separates officially confirmed information from rumors. Information can change if Rockstar announces new details.
